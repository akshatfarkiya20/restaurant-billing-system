## Problem

Small restaurants, cafés and canteens often prepare bills by hand or with a basic calculator. This is slow and leads to mistakes in adding prices, multiplying quantities and calculating GST, especially when the place is busy. Customers may receive wrong bills, and owners lose time and trust.

## Proposed Solution

A Python command-line application that stores the menu and prices, lets the user add or remove items with quantities, validates every input, and automatically generates an itemised bill with subtotal, 5% GST and the final total.

## Objectives

- Build a menu-driven billing program in Python.
- Manage a cart with add, remove, view and quantity control.
- Validate all user input to avoid errors.
- Calculate subtotal, GST and grand total automatically.
- Print a clear, readable bill.

## Scope

- Suitable for small eateries with a fixed menu, on a single terminal.
- Handles one order at a time.
- Does not store orders permanently or support multiple users (possible future work).

## Expected Outcome

A fast, accurate and easy-to-use billing tool that removes manual calculation errors and can later be extended with a graphical interface and database storage.
